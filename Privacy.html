Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 01 January 2026

At StudentHub, we value and respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and disclose your information when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

a) Personal Information

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Mobile Number
  • Student ID (if applicable)
  • Educational Preferences
  • Information submitted through forms

b) Non-Personal Information

  • Browser Type
  • Device Information
  • IP Address
  • Pages Visited
  • Cookies and Usage Data

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide student portal services.
  • Manage student accounts.
  • Display academic information.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Improve website performance.
  • Send important notifications.

3. Cookies

StudentHub may use cookies to improve your browsing experience, remember user preferences, and analyze website usage.

4. Data Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure. However, no online system is completely secure.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Your information may only be shared:

  • To comply with legal requirements.
  • With authorized service providers.
  • To protect StudentHub and its users.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. StudentHub is not responsible for their privacy practices.

7. Children's Privacy

StudentHub does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated effective date.
